The operating principle

AI reads and prepares. Rules calculate and control. People approve.

Capacity should never come from giving software uncontrolled authority. PRISM separates the three kinds of work so every dispute moves faster without loosening a single control.

AI

Reads and prepares

Reads EOBs, summarizes payer replies, drafts notices, assembles evidence packets and explains every recommendation with its source.

Rules

Calculates and controls

Controls eligibility checks, business-day deadlines, arithmetic, case state, retries and versioned decisions — deterministically, every time.

People

Decide and approve

Approve eligibility, settlements, filings, offers, rule changes and case closure. Named owners, recorded approvals, full audit history.

Human control

A clear automation boundary.

The safest design draws a hard line between automatic preparation and consequential decisions. Here is exactly where PRISM draws it.

Automatic preparation

  • Receive, scan, classify and match files
  • Detect duplicates and missing fields
  • Calculate clocks, balances and opportunity
  • Draft notices, summaries and evidence
  • Create queues, reminders and audit events

Recorded approval

  • Confirm eligibility and jurisdiction
  • Send a notice or payer response
  • Accept, reject or counter a settlement
  • Initiate IDR and select an IDRE
  • Submit evidence, offers or rule changes

Never autonomous

  • Invent evidence, dates, codes or amounts
  • Change live rules without approval
  • Send PHI to consumer AI accounts
  • Scrape licensed data or bypass controls
  • Autonomously submit to the CMS Gateway

Ten mandatory human gates across the case

  • 1Resolve uncertain data
  • 2Confirm eligibility
  • 3Send negotiation notice
  • 4Approve settlement
  • 5Initiate IDR
  • 6Select IDRE
  • 7Approve evidence
  • 8Submit final offer
  • 9Activate rule change
  • 10Close the case

Technology

Agents don't get open access. They get a gateway.

Every action an agent takes passes through an allow-listed internal function. There is no general-purpose tool use, no free-form browsing, and no path to an external system that skips the approval record.

Intake & connections

Secure portal · SFTP · authenticated APIs · X12 835/837 parser · approved mailbox · malware scan · OCR

Case & workflow

Rules & services

PHI-safe logs, DLP, SIEM, monitoring, manual fallback and a kill switch.

Change control

Rule, model, prompt and SOP versions with test results and rollback.

Important operating notes

  • PRISM publicly states HIPAA compliant and SOC 2 ready. Readiness is not the same as a completed independent audit.
  • HHS guidance treats a cloud provider handling ePHI as a business associate, requiring a HIPAA-compliant BAA.
  • CMS states the late-2026 IDR Gateway will permit only U.S.-based users. Submission stays supervised by authorized U.S.-based staff.
