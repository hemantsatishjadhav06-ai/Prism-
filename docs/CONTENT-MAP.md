## Claims that carry compliance weight

These are the statements a reviewer should read closely. All three come
straight from the proposal and are **deliberately conservative**.

### 1. Readiness ≠ audit

> "PRISM publicly states HIPAA compliant and SOC 2 ready. Readiness is not the
> same as a completed independent audit."

[PROP p.10]. This is on the page on purpose. Procurement teams check, and
stating it first is worth more than the alternative.

### 2. Cloud vendors handling ePHI are business associates

> "HHS guidance treats a cloud provider handling ePHI as a business associate,
> requiring a HIPAA-compliant BAA."

[PROP p.10], sourced to [HHS — HIPAA and Cloud Computing][hhs].

### 3. The CMS Gateway is U.S.-users-only

> "CMS states the late-2026 IDR Gateway will permit only U.S.-based users.
> Submission stays supervised by authorized U.S.-based staff."

[PROP p.10, p.14], sourced to the [CMS 2026 Federal IDR Operations Final
Rule][cms-rule].

## Claims deliberately NOT made

The proposal is explicit that percentages must not be promised before a pilot
is measured [PROP p.12], and the page honours that:

- **No recovery percentage, uplift figure, or win rate.** The measurement
  section publishes the *formula* and the metric families instead.
- **No time-saving claim** ("cuts prep time by X%"). The benefit lines describe
  direction ("less re-keying", "fewer preventable deadline losses"), never
  magnitude.
- **No customer names, logos or testimonials.** None were supplied.
- **No autonomy claim.** Every agent card names the human who signs. The
  "never autonomous" column exists specifically so the page cannot be read as
  promising unattended filing.

If marketing wants magnitude claims later, they come from the 30-day
diagnostic's measured baseline — that is what the diagnostic is for.

change what the product does:

- Exact federal and state rule coverage, effective dates, extensions and client
  approval authority.
- Rights to use internal outcomes, licensed benchmarks and client data for
  model evaluation.
- The live CMS Gateway operating model, supported interfaces and U.S.-based
  user requirements.
