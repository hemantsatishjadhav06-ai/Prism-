Human control

Where your team signs — and what the AI never touches.

The limits below are enforced in the tool gateway, not in a prompt. An agent cannot reach any external system until an approval is recorded against the current case version.

Ten mandatory human gates

  • 1Resolve uncertain data
  • 6Select the IDRE
  • 2Confirm eligibility
  • 7Approve the evidence
  • 3Send negotiation notice
  • 8Submit the final offer
  • 4Approve settlement
  • 9Activate a rule change
  • 5Initiate IDR
  • 10Close the case

What happens next

  • 0130-day diagnostic: confirm the real workflow, data and baseline KPIs.
  • 02Pick one pilot segment — one specialty, one or two payers.
  • 03Agents run in shadow mode; their output is compared to specialist decisions.
  • 04Expand only after accuracy, security and workflow gates pass.

Five things the AI will never do

  • Invent evidence, dates, codes or amounts
  • Change live rules without approval
  • Send PHI to consumer AI accounts
  • Scrape licensed data or bypass controls
  • Autonomously submit to the CMS Gateway

Recorded on every important action

Source document · case version · rule version · model and prompt · reviewer · timestamp.

No percentage of recovery or time saved is claimed here. Those come from your own measured pilot — that is what the diagnostic is for.
