The flow

One dispute, end to end.

Every column is a stage of the case. Blue is what the AI prepares. Amber is the human signature that unlocks the next step. Green is what comes out the other side.

AI prepares A person approves Result
STAGE 01

Intake

AI prepares · Agent 1

  • Reads EOBs, remits and support files
  • Extracts claim fields and matches service lines
  • Flags anything missing, duplicated or low-confidence

Gate 1 · you decide

  • Resolve every uncertain field before the case advances

Result

  • One verified case record, no re-keying
STAGE 02

Qualify

AI prepares · Agents 2, 3, 4

  • Applies federal, state, plan and network rules, with the reason attached
  • Starts the business-day clocks and the exception queue
  • Compares QPA to benchmarks and scores expected recovery

Gates 2–3 · you decide

  • Confirm eligibility and jurisdiction
  • Own any contested date

Result

  • A prioritized, eligible case on a live clock
STAGE 03

Negotiate

AI prepares · Agent 5

  • Drafts the Open Negotiation notice
  • Summarizes payer replies as they land
  • Compares settlement options side by side

Gates 4–5 · you decide

  • Send the notice
  • Accept, reject or counter a settlement

Result

  • Settlement — or escalation to IDR on day 31
STAGE 04

IDR

AI prepares · Agents 6, 7

  • Builds cited statements, exhibit index and attachment checks
  • Surfaces comparable outcomes by payer, code and IDRE
  • Runs the reviewer checklist before anything is submitted

Gates 6–8 · you decide

  • Initiate IDR and select the IDRE
  • Approve the evidence
  • Submit the final offer

Result

  • A filed dispute with a defensible packet
STAGE 05

Collect

AI prepares · Agents 8, 10

  • Matches the determination to the remittance
  • Flags late, partial or missing payment
  • Builds client, operations and executive reporting

Gates 9–10 · you decide

  • Confirm disputed balances
  • Close the case — never before the cash lands

Result

  • Cash received, reconciled and reported

The hard limits

Five things the AI will never do.

These are enforced in the tool gateway, not in a prompt. An agent cannot reach an external system at all until an approval is recorded against the current case version.

  • Invent evidence, dates, codes or amounts

  • Change live rules without approval

  • Send PHI to consumer AI accounts

  • Scrape licensed data or bypass controls

  • Autonomously submit to the CMS Gateway
